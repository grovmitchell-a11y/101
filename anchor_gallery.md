## UNIT 001 — FERGANA VILOYAT (UZ) · scored 2026-10-04

**UNIT:** Fergana viloyat, Republic of Uzbekistan (ADM1, admitted as honorary county-scale per Ruling K). ~6,800 km².

**Polygon definition:** the southern rim of the Fergana Valley — the Sokh, Shakhimardan-say, Margilan-say and Isfayram fan belt plus the Kokand oasis: Kokand (Qo'qon), Margilan, Fergana city (f. 1876 as New Skobelev/New Margilan), Rishtan, Quva (medieval Quba), Besharik, Altyarik, etc. Includes the durable exclaves Sokh and Shakhimardan inside Kyrgyzstan (post-1955 boundary state; no ≤1-year divergences used, per Ruling K). Roughly the Tsarist Kokand + Margilan uezds; ~25% of the whole valley's modern population, lawyered to 30–35% of its pre-modern population on fan-oasis area (see Allocation Registry).

**PEAK WINDOW + moment:** Window 7, High medieval, **c. 1000–1020 CE** (pre-Qarakhanid-war, pre-Mongol florescence). Contested with Window 4 (c. 110 BCE, Dayuan) and Window 11 (c. 1817, Kokand khanate) — three-way ambiguity reported per procedure.

**WINNING PATHWAY:** GDP (pop share × argued 1.2–1.3 commercial multiplier, Kashgar-road belt). Population pathway nearly ties.

**LAWYERED SHARE:** GDP **0.13–0.18%** of world; population **0.11–0.14%** (c. 1000–1020). Co-contenders: c. 110 BCE pop 0.06–0.12%; c. 1817 GDP 0.09–0.14% (Ruling A multiplier on khanate fiscal documentation).

**CONSENSUS SHARE:** ~0.04–0.06% at the same moment (valley ~550–700k, polygon ~25%, world ~280M). Consensus ALL-TIME peak is arguably **today**: ~4.0M of ~8.1B = **0.050%** population (2024).

**DELTA:** ≈ **3×** (high end 3.5×).

**BAND:** **10** (lawyered, top-of-range per Ruling I; all three candidate windows clear 0.09% at their tops, so the band survives the collapse of any two). Consensus band: **8–9**, straddling the 0.05% line.

### Numerator components (with citations)
- **c. 1000–1020:** the 10th-c. geographic corpus (al-Istakhri, Ibn Hawqal, al-Muqaddasi; Hudud al-'Alam) credits Farghana with on the order of forty minbar-towns — one of the densest town rosters in Islamdom, endorsed as exceptional by Barthold (*Turkestan Down to the Mongol Invasion*). Valley construction: ~40 minbar-towns at high-range 10–25k each + rural hinterland at 2.5–3 persons per irrigated ha over the attested medieval canal command → **0.95–1.2M valley**; polygon allocation 30% (Quba ranked by the geographers just behind the capital Akhsikath; Margilan, Rishtan, Khavakand/Khuqand all attested 10th-c.; the southern fans are the valley's largest) → **285–360k**. Multiplier 1.2–1.3 ("argued" commercial tier): the Kashgar road ran through the polygon (Khuqand–Margilan–Quba–Osh–Irkeshtam), with attested sericulture (Margilan) and ceramics (Rishtan).
- **c. 110 BCE (Dayuan):** Shiji 123 — some seventy walled towns, population "several tens of myriads" (數十萬), maximal reading ~400–500k; Hanshu 96A as usually read: 60,000 households, 300,000 persons, 60,000 bowmen. Valley 300–500k × 30% polygon → **90–150k**. Multiplier 1.0–1.2 (heavenly-horse export to Han argued) → GDP 0.06–0.13%.
- **c. 1817 (Kokand khanate, Umar Khan r. c. 1810–1822):** polygon ~450–550k (Kokand city ~80k per 19th-c. visitors, anchored by the 1897 census figure of ~82k; Margilan ~30k; dense irrigated countryside, 1897 backcast). Multiplier 2.0–2.5 under the "quantified fiscal/commercial documentation" tier, named sources: the Kokand khanate archive (Troitskaya's 1968 catalogue); the Qing–Kokand trade corpus (Fletcher in *Cambridge History of China* vol. 10 — the 1832 settlement as China's "first unequal treaty"; Newby, *The Empire and the Khanate*, 2005). Kokand farmed customs on the Altishahr trade and ran the Russia–Kashgar transit. GDP **0.09–0.14%**.

### Denominator (with citation)
McEvedy & Jones floors per rubric table, log-linear per Ruling F: 265M at 1000 CE (window 6/7 join); ~143M at 110 BCE (window 4 interpolation, below the 160M 1-CE anchor); ~0.99B at 1817 (window 11); 8.1B at 2024. ### Veto notes (claims killed)
1. "Kokand khanate = 5–6M" (high pre-conquest Russian guesses) — killed for polygon use: khanate ≠ polygon, and the 1897 census (Kokand + Margilan uezds ≈ 680k) caps any backcast.
2. 數十萬 stretched to ">1M valley in 100 BCE" — killed by pre-Kushan canal extent vs carrying capacity (survey evidence: ancient command area well under the medieval one).
3. al-Farghani (Alfraganus) as Mind 8 — killed by the event-fame ban: a (contested) birthplace claim; the *Elements of Astronomy* was written in Baghdad. Dante and Columbus cite the Baghdad book, not the valley.
4. "Margilan founded by Alexander" — legend, no structural weight.
5. Great Fergana Canal (1939, ~160k farmers, ~45 days) as Strategy 8 — killed: heroic mobilization, regional infrastructure, no global chokepoint.

### Qualitative dimensions
- **Strategy & resources: 7** — nameable: the 1832 Qing–Kokand settlement, a Kokand-run customs/consular (aqsaqal) regime inside Qing Altishahr (Fletcher's "first unequal treaty"); secondary: sole external remount source of "heavenly horses" for Han cavalry c. 100 BCE; ~1/5 of Russian Empire cotton by 1913; Soviet cotton core. Below Suez (9): regional leverage, not a world chokepoint.
- **Mind & meaning: 6** — the Kokand court literary school (Umar Khan, the poetess Nodira, later Muqimi and Furqat), ~100+ madrasas claimed in 19th-c. Kokand; Margilan atlas-ikat and Rishtan ceramics as living craft lineages. No ≥8 claim survives the event-fame ban.
