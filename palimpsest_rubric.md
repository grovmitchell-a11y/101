# PALIMPSEST RUBRIC v1.0 (+ Locked Rulings E–K)

Score the ALL-TIME PEAK global importance of one modern county-scale unit (US county / EU NUTS-3 / ADM2 equivalent). Score the modern polygon retroactively: everything that ever happened inside today's boundary counts, whoever drew it. Band 1–10 = a lookup on the lawyered peak share; the share is the real output.

## PROCEDURE (in order)
1. **Timeline sketch** — one line per window (all 16), rough share arithmetic only. Flag 1–3 candidate peak windows.
2. **Deep-score candidates only.** For each: build the lawyered numerator, take the floor denominator AT THE SAME YEAR, compute share for both pathways (pop, GDP). Check qualitative dimensions (Strategy, Mind) against pegs.
3. **Comparative check** — before finalizing, place the unit between two adjacent anchors from the gallery ("above X's peak, below Y's").
4. **Band** via the ladder. Overall = max across windows × pathways/dimensions.
5. **Output** per the schema. Report ambiguity (contested peak windows) openly; never suppress it.

## LAWYER RULES
- **Numerator (maximalist):** the highest figure defended in credentialed print — any era of scholarship, minority and 19th-c. maximalist schools admissible — or a direct reading of a primary source some scholars take seriously. Ancient sources are innocent until proven guilty; this applies symmetrically to Herodotus, Ibn Hawqal, Marco Polo, and Qing gazetteers alike.
- **Physical veto:** no figure survives contradiction by hard constraints — walled/built area × max attested density, hinterland carrying capacity + attested imports, survey and necropolis evidence. Record what the veto killed.
- **Denominator (minimalist):** use the floors table. Going below the floor requires a citation. Numerator and denominator must be taken at the same year (the claimed peak moment).
- **Equivalence rule:** societies without surviving texts get the HIGH end of published archaeological ranges. Non-literacy must not be penalized.
- **Event-fame ban:** score structural role only. A famous battle, birth, or treaty is not importance.
- **No double counting** of population/GDP across adjacent units. No pseudo-precision before 1500: report ranges.

## GDP PATHWAY
- Post-1820: Maddison series; contemporary: current-dollar shares of ~$107T world.
- Pre-1820: GDP share = pop share × multiplier, per this schedule:
  - 0.8–1.2 default agrarian
  - 1.2–1.6 regional commercial/administrative center (argued)
  - 1.6–2.0 imperial capital or major entrepôt (argued)
  - 2.0–2.5 quantified fiscal/commercial documentation (named source required)
  - >2.5 only with extraction-grade quantified records (registered mine output, customs/company ledgers, papyrological accounts), valued at world prices over a Maddison-consistent world GDP proxy.

## QUALITATIVE DIMENSIONS (Strategy & resources; Mind & meaning)
Peg-based, no arithmetic. Any score ≥8 requires a nameable claim (a specific institution, commodity, chokepoint, command). Can reach 10 regardless of mass. Seed pegs: Makkah = 10 (Mind); Los Alamos Co. 1945 = 10 (Mind/Strategy); Suez = 9 (Strategy). Append every finalized unit to the anchor gallery.

## LOCKED RULINGS v1.0
A. Documented commerce pierces the 2.5× cap (customs/company ledgers = extraction-equivalent).
B. Extraction-GDP = registered output × world prices ÷ world GDP proxy.
C. Qualitative dimensions may reach 10 with negligible mass.
D. Window ties: max share is the record; earliest attainment and duration go in metadata.

## LOCKED RULINGS E–K (session 2026-10-04, commissioner assenting)
E. **Coherence patch.** When the lawyered numerator implies a regional total above the floor's regional component (e.g. Berkeley-school central Mexico vs the M&J Americas allocation), the overhang is added to the denominator. Kills only self-refuting ratios.
F. **Interpolation.** Floors between window endpoints are log-linear; named anchors (1 CE ≈160M, plague troughs) override. A window-boundary year takes the LOWER of the two windows' values (e.g. 1450 = 350M, not 375M).
G. **Pre-1820 world GDP proxy** (for Ruling B): floor population × $450 per head (1990 GK$, Maddison's near-subsistence world mean), or the Maddison benchmark world total where one exists and is lower.
H. **Contemporary window:** per unit, take max(market share of ~$107T, PPP share of the PPP world total). House convention preserved: divide by $107T, convert shares to 2025 dollars at $127T.
I. **Range banding.** The band reads the top of the lawyered range; the consensus share and DELTA are always co-reported.
J. **Legacy ledger** (`city_ledger_corrected.csv`, 269 city snapshots) is grandfathered as legacy anchors, flagged, re-scored on contact with the new polygon discipline.
K. **Commissioner's rulings:** units and boundaries are as the commissioner names them (Uzbek viloyats admitted as honorary county-scale); boundary divergences lasting ≤1 year are ignored; lowest defensible world totals affirmed as house policy.
